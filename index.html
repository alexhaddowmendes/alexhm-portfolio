About Me

A driven senior technical game designer with around ten years in the games industry. I started out as a hobbyist, making mods and custom levels in level editors, and grew into a professional collaborator working with renowned developers such as Ubisoft and Rockstar Games. With a first-class honours degree in Games Design and expertise in Unreal Engine, Unity, Godot, and various programming languages, I excel in designing and prototyping innovative gameplay experiences.
